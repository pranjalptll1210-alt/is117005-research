# Portfolio Research — Career Exploration

**Research question:** What do technology and project management employers look for, and how can I show those skills through my portfolio?

**Research date:** November 11, 2026

## Career 1: Steelcase — Information Technology Summer Intern

Source: https://careers.steelcase.com/external/JobDescription?folderId=10705&source=Smart+Recruiter

This internship focuses on technology, business systems, problem-solving, and collaboration.

**Evidence:** The employer describes opportunities to work with technology teams and gain hands-on experience.

**My finding:** Employers want students who can apply technical knowledge to practical business problems.

**Portfolio decision:** I want to feature my IT Asset Analytics Tracker project. It can demonstrate technical skills, problem-solving, and business analysis.

**Planned check:** Ask a classmate to review my project description and explain what business problem it solves.

## Career 2: AECOM Tishman — Assistant Construction Project Manager

Source: https://jobs.smartrecruiters.com/AECOM2/744000160737109-assistant-construction-project-manager-aecom-tishman

This position focuses on project scheduling, coordination, cost monitoring, and documentation.

**Evidence:** The employer describes responsibilities related to managing project information and coordinating work.

**My finding:** Project management requires organization, communication, and the ability to track progress.

**Portfolio decision:** Add a project worklog that shows milestones, decisions, and progress.

**Planned check:** Ask a classmate to follow one documented milestone and identify its supporting evidence.

## Comparison

Both positions value problem-solving, organization, and communication.

Steelcase focuses more directly on technology and information systems. AECOM Tishman emphasizes construction project management and coordination.

My current portfolio direction is business technology, with project management as a secondary interest.
