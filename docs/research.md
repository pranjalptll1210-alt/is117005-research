# Where AI career opportunities are growing

<div class="research-label">IS117005 · Research question</div>

**Which careers connected to AI show credible growth signals, what work do they involve, and how can a student prepare?**

## Main findings

1. **Data science has a strong official growth outlook.** BLS projects 35% U.S. employment growth from 2025 to 2035. Its work includes analyzing data and developing models to support decisions. [BLS: Data Scientists](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/math/data-scientists.htm)
2. **AI research and security provide additional routes.** BLS projects 22% growth for computer and information research scientists and 21% for information security analysts over the same period. Neither category is exclusively AI. [BLS: Research Scientists](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/computer-and-information-research-scientists.htm) · [BLS: Security Analysts](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/information-security-analysts.htm)
3. **Global employers expect AI and ML specialists to grow quickly.** The World Economic Forum lists them among the fastest-growing roles through 2030. This is an employer-survey outlook, distinct from the U.S. projections. [WEF: Jobs Outlook](https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/2-jobs-outlook/)
4. **New specialties reflect different stages of building AI.** Agent development, evaluation, operations, and governance involve distinct responsibilities. Their inclusion here is a research synthesis of technical practice, not evidence that each title has a measured growth rate.

Sources: the three BLS profiles and WEF chapter linked above, accessed November 5, 2026. Older 2024–2034 BLS figures appear in archived releases; this table uses the current 2025–2035 profiles consistently.

## What “new job title” means

A title can be new while much of its work is familiar. An **AI agent engineer** combines software engineering with model orchestration. An **LLMOps engineer** applies operations practices to language-model applications. An **AI governance analyst** brings risk assessment and documentation to AI systems. Employers may use different titles for similar responsibilities.

These are emerging specializations, not ten newly invented occupations. Read actual job descriptions for tasks, required experience, and ownership before choosing a career based on its name.

## Research limits

Growth is a projection, not a guarantee of employment. A growing occupation can still have competitive entry-level hiring. Employer surveys measure expectations and do not represent every employer. This project does not count live vacancies or claim that every listed specialty is among the fastest-growing jobs. Portfolio suggestions and entry routes are educational recommendations, not hiring requirements.
