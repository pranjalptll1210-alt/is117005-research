# What real NY/NJ postings reveal

**Reviewed in Chrome on November 5, 2026.** This research expands the original career overview into employer-specific evidence. The [directory](/jobs) contains 21 requisitions, not a count of individual seats. Twenty have readable employer descriptions and an Apply control; one has employer-board confirmation with a secondary description.

## Coverage

| Experience bucket | Postings | Interpretation |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Internship | 5 | Explicit internships; some require graduate study |
| Entry-level | 4 | Three explicit new-grad roles and one inferred early-career infrastructure role with partial verification |
| Mid-level | 4 | Three-year experience requirements or equivalent |
| Experienced | 2 | Five-plus years required; no seniority stated in employer title |
| Senior / leadership | 5 | Explicit senior, staff, management, director, or lead roles |
| Unspecified | 1 | Employer gives no formal level or numeric threshold |

Six postings include NJ and sixteen include NY; the J&J multi-location internship counts in both, so geography totals overlap. Local places include New York City, Jersey City, Piscataway, Rahway, New Brunswick, and Princeton, plus the partially verified Livingston role. These are sample counts, not regional market shares.

## FDE is a family of jobs

**Forward Deployed Engineer** combines implementation with close contact with users. In this sample, [Google](/jobs#google-fde-intern), [Pace](/jobs#pace-fde-intern), and [Edra](/jobs#edra-fde) deploy solutions for customers; [Coinbase](/jobs#coinbase-hr-intern) applies the model to internal HR operations. The same title can therefore mean different customers, workflows, and technical depth.

## Systems and IT are genuine paths into AI

[Chalk’s new-grad role](/jobs#chalk-newgrad) emphasizes query engines, compilers, and systems. [Decagon’s graduate cohort](/jobs#decagon-newgrad) spans agents, orchestration, product, and infrastructure. Neither requires that every engineer train foundation models.

[Legora](/jobs#legora-workplace) shows a different pattern: established workplace IT with AI-assisted support duties. [CoreWeave](/jobs#coreweave-fleet) illustrates GPU-fleet operations, although its detailed requirements have a weaker verification status in this study. We label these adjacent or evolving roles rather than claiming they are newly invented occupations.

## Operations now includes managing models and agents

[Cloudflare’s Model Operations Lead](/jobs#cloudflare-model-ops) owns model launches, provider integration, catalog accuracy, and routing quality. [Armory’s Applied AI Strategist](/jobs#armory-strategist) focuses on redesigning healthcare workflows and making customer deployments succeed. These are distinct from conventional data science.

## Security and governance are different disciplines

[Ripple](/jobs#ripple-ai-security) and [MTA](/jobs#mta-ai-security) describe technical security work around AI systems. [Dentsu’s analyst and engineer roles](/jobs#dentsu-governance-analyst) separate evidence preparation from approval coordination and enterprise onboarding. The analyst title is not an entry-level signal: it requests five years of relevant experience.

## NJ offers more than NYC overflow

[GenScript in Piscataway](/jobs#genscript-agent) hires for enterprise agent integration. [Merck in Rahway](/jobs#merck-ai-comms-intern) offers AI adoption work for communications students. [J&J](/jobs#jnj-cv-intern) lists New Brunswick for a graduate-level medical AI internship. [Acadia in Princeton](/jobs#acadia-ai-director) seeks leadership for an enterprise agent platform, while [JPMorganChase in Jersey City](/jobs#jpm-agent-iii) seeks production agent engineering experience.

## What students should notice

- **A real new-grad label matters.** Chalk, Decagon, and Hyphenate explicitly define graduate eligibility; Hyphenate still requires an internship.
- **Internships vary sharply.** Pace says AI experience is not required; Google asks for substantial applied AI experience; J&J requires a master’s/PhD in progress.
- **Non-CS paths exist.** Merck’s AI communications internship focuses on testing, explanation, and adoption.
- **Missing experience years do not establish entry level.** Armory is left unclassified.
- **A posting proves a job exists, not that its title is growing rapidly.** Establishing growth would require a repeatable time series.

## What BLS adds—and cannot add

BLS provides broad occupational context, not a local FDE or agent-engineer vacancy count. Its [computer/IT overview](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/home.htm) projects about 280,000 annual openings nationally in 2025–2035, including replacement hiring.

Growth is not uniform. [Computer network architects](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/computer-network-architects.htm) are projected to grow **8%**, while [network and computer systems administrators](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/network-and-computer-systems-administrators.htm) are projected to decline **4%** over 2025–2035. An AI infrastructure posting does not mean every adjacent IT occupation is expanding. The existing [growth research](/research) covers data science, research scientists, and information security.

## What this review changes

The strongest conclusion is **breadth of AI-related work**, not a ranking of the fastest-growing new titles. The examples span deployment, systems, reliability, security, governance, and adoption. Existing engineering and business skills are being applied to new AI responsibilities.

For students, eligibility is a better starting point than a fashionable title. This sample contains **three explicit new-grad roles**, five internships with very different prerequisites, and one partially verified infrastructure role whose entry-level classification is an inference. The [student entry guide](/student-routes) compares those differences and separates employer requirements from suggested portfolio work.

The directory now separates **employer title seniority** from our **research experience group**. Dentsu’s five-year requirements justify an experienced grouping; they do not make the employer’s Analyst or Engineer title a Senior title.

## Questions the next research round must answer

1. **Junior access:** Are there directly verified undergraduate or new-grad options in AI security, governance, IT, and infrastructure? Their limited coverage here is a sampling gap, not evidence that those opportunities do not exist.
2. **Change over time:** Repeat a fixed set of searches with the same locations and inclusion criteria. Preserve requisition IDs, first/last seen dates, and closure status before comparing unique posting counts. Counts measure advertising activity, not hires or employment growth.
3. **Skills and barriers:** Record required versus preferred skills, degree eligibility, prior experience, office attendance, and restrictions in separate fields. Do not infer that an omitted requirement is absent.
4. **Regional coverage:** Expand employer coverage across NJ and NYC, including established employers and smaller firms, before making comparisons between the two locations.

This page is a synthesis of the existing November 5 snapshot; it does not represent a new check of employer availability. The dataset supports career exploration, not hiring probabilities, representative salary estimates, or a local growth ranking.
