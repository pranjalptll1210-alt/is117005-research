## Search terms for future research

The following are **research leads**, not additional verified openings: LLMOps engineer, inference engineer, GPU performance engineer, AI red-team engineer, AI platform SRE, agent reliability engineer, context engineer, AI FinOps analyst, AI enablement specialist, AI assurance analyst, and robotics/physical-AI engineer. Search these alongside city names and employer career pages. Do not count a title until its actual responsibilities and location have been checked.

## How this relates to BLS occupations

This is an interpretive mapping, not an official occupational-code assignment.

| Observed work | Useful BLS family to investigate |
| --- | --- |
| Building agents, APIs, and evaluation platforms | Software developers and software quality assurance |
| Assessing AI threats and controls | Information security analysts |
| Improving enterprise workflows | Computer systems analysts |
| Workplace troubleshooting | Computer support specialists |
| Cluster/network operations and design | Network and computer systems administrators; computer network architects |
| Developing new AI methods | Computer and information research scientists |

See the [BLS computer and IT occupational directory](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/home.htm). One employer title may span several families; do not transfer a broad growth percentage directly to the title.
